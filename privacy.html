Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 15, 2025

1. Data Collection

We collect only necessary information, including student academic records, parent contact details, and Aadhaar numbers for admissions and fee processing. All information is collected with parental consent and used solely for educational purposes.

2. Purpose of Use

Your data is used strictly for school administration, student safety, academic updates, fee management, and communication with parents. We never sell your data or use it for targeted advertising.

3. Data Security

All personal information is stored on secure servers with restricted access. We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, firewalls, and regular security audits to prevent unauthorized access or breaches.

4. Data Rights

Parents have the right to review, correct, or request the deletion of their child's information at any time by contacting the school office. We respond to all data requests within 30 days as per DPDP Act, 2023.

5. Data Retention

We keep your data only as long as the student is enrolled or as required by educational board regulations. After withdrawal, records are retained for 5 years as per legal requirements and then securely destroyed.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses essential cookies for functionality and security. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

7. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties except as required by law, for educational board requirements, or with explicit parental consent. All third-party service providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

8. Children's Privacy

We are committed to protecting children's privacy. All data collected is with parental consent. Student photos and achievements are shared only with parental permission. We comply with all child protection laws and regulations.

By enrolling your child at R.A. Bhageria Educational Academy, you consent to the collection and processing of your personal information as outlined in this Privacy Policy. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting the school office.
