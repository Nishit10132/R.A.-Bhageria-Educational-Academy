Withdrawal Procedure

Parents/Guardians wishing to withdraw their ward from the school are required to follow the procedure outlined below to ensure a smooth and timely process.

1

Submit Written Application

Parents/Guardians must submit a written request in the prescribed format to the Admin Executive for the issuance of the School Leaving Certificate (LC). The prescribed LC format can be accessed from the school website or obtained from the school office.

2

Clear All Dues

The LC will be processed only after all pending fees and dues of the student are fully cleared, if applicable. This includes tuition fees, library dues, sports fees, and any other outstanding payments.

3

Processing Time

A minimum of ten (10) working days is required to process and issue the LC after submission of the complete request. Please plan accordingly to avoid any delays.

4

Withdrawal During Academic Year

In case the LC is requested during the academic year, the tuition fee for the ongoing quarter must be paid in full before the LC is issued.

5

Withdrawal at End of Academic Year

For withdrawal at the end of the academic year, the LC application must be submitted on or before the end of February. Applications received after this period will require payment of Quarter 1 fees (April, May, and June).

Documents Required for Withdrawal

  • Duly filled withdrawal application form
  • Original fee receipt book (if applicable)
  • Identity card of the student
  • Library clearance certificate
  • Sports equipment return confirmation
  • No dues certificate from all departments

Important Guidelines

  • LC will be issued only after all dues are cleared and documents are submitted.
  • Withdrawal application once submitted cannot be cancelled without administrative approval.
  • Fees once paid are non-refundable except for security deposit (if applicable).
  • LC will be handed over only to parents/legal guardians with valid ID proof.
  • For any clarification regarding withdrawal, please contact the school admission office.

Fee Refund Policy

Within 15 Days

Full refund of fees (excluding admission fee) if withdrawal is requested within 15 days of session start.

Within 1 Month

50% refund of tuition fee (excluding admission fee) if withdrawal is requested within first month.

After 1 Month

No refund of fees. Only security deposit (if applicable) will be refunded after deducting dues.

For assistance with withdrawal procedure:

Contact Admission Office
