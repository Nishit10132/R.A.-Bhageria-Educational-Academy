Age Criteria for Admission

Note: The following age requirements are based on the guidelines issued in the Government Gazette. These criteria must be met as per the admission year 2026-27.

Important Guidelines

  • The age criteria are strictly followed as per government norms.
  • Date of Birth should be as per the Birth Certificate issued by Municipal Corporation.
  • No relaxation in age criteria will be granted under any circumstances.
  • For Class XI and XII, admission is based on previous class results and stream availability.
  • For any queries regarding age criteria, please contact the school admission office.
