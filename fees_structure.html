Payment Information

Online Payment

Pay via Net Banking, Credit/Debit Card, UPI, or NACH mandate

Offline Payment

Demand Draft (DD) or Cash at school office during working hours

Payment Schedule

Quarterly payments due in April, July, October, and January

Fee Concession

Available for RTE, EWS, Staff Children, and Meritorious Students

Important Guidelines

  • Fees are to be paid on or before the due date to avoid late payment charges.
  • Admission fee is non-refundable and payable only at the time of new admission.
  • Transport fee, if applicable, will be charged separately based on route distance.
  • Fee structure is subject to revision as per the school management's decision.
  • For detailed fee structure and concession details, please contact the school admission office.
