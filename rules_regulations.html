Rules & Regulations

Guidelines for students, parents, and staff to maintain discipline and harmony

School Leaving Certificate (LC) Rules

  • LC will be issued only after clearance of all dues.
  • Written application by parents is mandatory.
  • LC will be issued within 7 working days.
  • Transfer during the academic year is discouraged.
  • Original documents will be returned after LC issuance.

Fees Rules & Regulations

  • Fees must be paid on or before the 10th of every month.
  • Fees once paid are non-refundable under any circumstances.
  • Late payment of ₹50 per day will be charged after due date.
  • Clearance of all dues is mandatory for exams, report cards, and certificates.
  • Prolonged non-payment (3 months) may lead to removal from school.

Parents / Guardians Rules

  • Provide accurate details during admission; false info may cancel admission.
  • Ensure regular attendance and punctuality; inform school in case of absence.
  • Communicate respectfully with teachers & staff at all times.
  • Attend Parent-Teacher Meetings regularly (held every 2 months).
  • Support school rules, discipline & uniform standards.

School Rules & Code of Conduct

  • Wear full, clean uniform daily & carry school ID card.
  • No nail polish, hair colour, cosmetics, or extreme hairstyles.
  • Maintain silence & discipline in classrooms & corridors.
  • Respect school property; no writing or damage to walls/furniture.
  • Mobile phones & electronic gadgets are strictly banned.

Admissions & Enrollment Guidelines

  • Application: Complete application with all required documents is mandatory.
  • Documents: Photo, birth certificate, TC/School Leaving Certificate required.
  • Eligibility: Admission depends on age, readiness & seat availability.
  • Fees: Fees must be paid on time to continue academic access.
  • Withdrawal: Written notice and dues clearance required for TC.

General Discipline

  • Punctuality is essential; latecomers will be marked absent.
  • Use of foul language or misbehavior is strictly prohibited.
  • Bullying, ragging, or any form of harassment is not tolerated.
  • Students must respect teachers and staff at all times.
  • Maintain cleanliness in classrooms and campus.

Attendance & Punctuality

  • Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory for appearing in exams.
  • Parents must inform school in writing for any absence.
  • Medical certificate required for absence due to illness (more than 3 days).
  • Repeated absence without notice may lead to removal of name.
  • Students must arrive by 7:50 AM; latecomers will be marked absent.

Library Rules

  • Maintain complete silence in the library.
  • Books must be returned on time; fine of ₹5 per day for late return.
  • Damaged or lost books must be replaced or paid double the price.
  • Reference books cannot be taken out of the library.
  • Library card is mandatory for borrowing books.

Laboratory Rules

  • Follow all safety instructions given by the teacher.
  • Handle equipment with care; report any damage immediately.
  • No eating or drinking inside the lab.
  • Wear appropriate safety gear during experiments (lab coat, gloves, goggles).
  • Do not touch chemicals or equipment without teacher's permission.

Transport Rules

  • Be at the bus stop 5 minutes before scheduled time.
  • No unruly behavior inside the bus.
  • Do not lean out of the bus window.
  • Students must board and alight only at designated stops.
  • Bus fees must be paid on time to retain transport facility.

Uniform Guidelines

  • Full uniform must be worn on all working days.
  • ID card must be worn and visible at all times.
  • Sports uniform for physical education classes only.
  • Students without proper uniform will be sent back home.
  • Black shoes with white socks mandatory.
