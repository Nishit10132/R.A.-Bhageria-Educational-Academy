Transport Facility

Safe, reliable and well-organized transport system for students

Our school provides a safe, reliable and well-organized transport system to ensure that students travel comfortably to and from the campus. The fleet is regularly maintained, equipped with proper safety measures and driven by trained and experienced drivers.

Safety & Supervision

Vehicles monitored for speed, route adherence. Students supervised during travel.

Comfortable Travel

Spacious, clean and well-ventilated vehicles for a comfortable ride.

Well-Planned Routes

Routes covering major residential areas efficiently with minimal travel time.

Punctuality & Reliability

Fixed schedules and timely updates for pick-up and drop-off timings.

Transport Guidelines

Be at bus stop 5 minutes before scheduled time
No unruly behavior inside the bus
Do not lean out of the bus window
Board and alight only at designated stops
Transport fees must be paid on time
Bus pass must be carried daily
