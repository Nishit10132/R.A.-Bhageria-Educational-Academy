Business Ordinance Clause

Last Updated: March 15, 2025

1. Governing Entity

R.A. Bhageria Educational Academy is managed and operated by Lala Govindramjee Charitable Society, New Delhi, a registered trust under the Society Registration Act, 1860. All business transactions, admissions, and operations are conducted under the purview of this trust.

2. Legal Compliance & Governing Law

All business operations, transactions, and activities of the school are governed by the laws of the Republic of India. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with the use of school services or this website shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Bharuch, Gujarat. The school complies with all applicable central and state education acts, including:

  • ✓ Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009 (RTE)
  • ✓ Gujarat Self-Financed School (Regulation of Fees) Act
  • ✓ Information Technology Act, 2000
  • ✓ Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)

3. Tax Compliance & Registration

The school operates with full tax compliance under Indian tax laws. All applicable taxes, including GST, are collected and deposited as per statutory requirements. Parents are provided with valid tax receipts for all payments. The trust's PAN and registration details are available upon request.

4. Non-Commercial Use

This website and its portals are provided exclusively for the educational and administrative use of students and parents. Users are strictly prohibited from using any part of this website for commercial solicitation, advertising, or business promotion without prior written consent from the School Management.

5. Accuracy of Transactions

All financial transactions, including fee payments, must be made in accordance with the school's official fee schedule. The School is not liable for any losses arising from payments made to unauthorized third parties or through fraudulent links not provided on this official domain. Users are advised to verify all payment details before completing transactions.

6. Anti-Fraud & Compliance

Users agree to provide true and accurate information for all business interactions with the school. Any attempt to bypass security, use unauthorized payment methods, or provide forged documents will result in immediate termination of website access and potential legal action under the Indian Penal Code and Information Technology Act, 2000.

7. Service Availability & Liability

The School reserves the right to suspend online services (fee portals, parent dashboards) for maintenance or due to technical emergencies. The School shall not be held liable for any business loss or late fees incurred due to temporary service outages. Users are encouraged to complete payments before the due date to avoid any last-minute issues.

8. Intellectual Property

All content on this website, including the school name, logo, curriculum, and digital materials, is the exclusive property of Lala Govindramjee Charitable Society. Unauthorized reproduction, distribution, or commercial use of any content is strictly prohibited and may result in legal action.

9. Modification of Business Terms

The School Management reserves the right to modify, amend, or update these business terms and conditions at any time without prior notice. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting on this page. Continued use of school services constitutes acceptance of the modified terms.

10. Force Majeure

The School shall not be liable for any delay or failure in performance of its obligations due to events beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters, government actions, pandemic, civil unrest, or technical failures beyond the school's control.

By enrolling your child at R.A. Bhageria Educational Academy and using this website, you acknowledge and agree to abide by all the above business ordinance clauses. The school management reserves the right to take appropriate legal action against any violation of these terms. For any business-related queries, please contact the school office.
