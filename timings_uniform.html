School Timings & Uniform

Guidelines for punctuality and dress code to maintain discipline and identity

School Timings

School timings may vary for different classes. Parents are requested to ensure punctual arrival and departure of students. Any changes in timings will be communicated in advance.

Grade Timings Saturday
Nursery to UKG 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM Holiday
Class I to X 8:00 AM to 1:35 PM 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM

School Uniform

Wearing the official school uniform is mandatory. Students should ensure the uniform is clean, complete and worn as per school guidelines.

Boys Uniform

Nursery to Class IV: Light Blue Shirt with White Checks, Navy Blue Shorts, Belt, Tie, Navy Blue Socks, Black Shoes
Class V onwards: Light Blue Shirt with White Checks, Navy Blue Full Pant, Belt, Tie, Navy Blue Socks, Black Shoes

Girls Uniform

Nursery to Class IV: Light Blue Shirt with White Checks, Navy Blue Skirt, Belt, Tie, Navy Blue Socks, Black Shoes
Class V onwards: Light Blue Shirt with White Checks, Navy Blue Skirt, Belt, Tie, Navy Blue Socks, Black Shoes

Uniform Guidelines

Uniform must be clean, ironed, and worn properly
ID card must be worn and visible at all times
Sports uniform for physical education classes only
Nails should be trimmed and kept clean
Hair neatly combed; boys short, girls tied properly
Black shoes with white socks mandatory
School sweater/cardigan during winter season
No nail polish, hair colour, or cosmetics
