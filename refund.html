Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: March 15, 2025

1. Non-Refundable Fees

Admission fee, registration charges, and term fees for the current quarter are strictly non-refundable under any circumstances. These fees are collected to process admission and secure the seat for your child.

2. Withdrawal Before Session Start

If a parent wishes to withdraw admission before the academic session begins, a written application must be submitted to the Principal. In such cases:

  • ✓ 100% refund of tuition fee (excluding admission fee) if withdrawal 30+ days before session start
  • ✓ 50% refund of tuition fee if withdrawal 15-30 days before session start
  • ✓ No refund if withdrawal less than 15 days before session start

3. Withdrawal During Academic Session

For withdrawals during the academic session:

  • ✓ Full refund of tuition fee (excluding admission fee) if withdrawal within 15 days of session start
  • ✓ 50% refund of tuition fee if withdrawal within 30 days of session start
  • ✓ No refund if withdrawal after 30 days of session start

4. Transport Fee Refund

Transport fee refund is available only if requested before the transport service commences. Once the transport service has started for the term, no refund will be provided. Changes in route or stop may be considered subject to availability.

5. Transaction Charges

Online convenience fees, service taxes, or payment gateway transaction charges paid at the time of fee deposition are non-refundable under any circumstances, as these are collected by third-party service providers and cannot be reversed.

6. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds will be processed via the original payment method (Credit Card/UPI/Bank Account) within 10 to 15 working days from the date of approval. The actual credit time may vary depending on your bank's internal policies.

7. Security Deposit Refund

Security deposit (if applicable) is refundable at the time of final withdrawal, subject to:

  • ✓ Clearance of all outstanding dues
  • ✓ Return of all school property (library books, uniform, etc.)
  • ✓ Submission of withdrawal application as per policy

8. Termination of Services

The school reserves the right to cancel a student's enrollment or access to online portals in cases of non-payment of fees, violation of the school's code of conduct, or disciplinary issues. In such cases, no refund will be provided.

9. Fee Concession Cancellation

Any fee concession granted is valid only for the current academic year. If the student withdraws, the concession is automatically canceled. The school reserves the right to review and cancel concessions based on performance or policy changes.

By enrolling your child at R.A. Bhageria Educational Academy, you acknowledge and agree to abide by all the above refund and cancellation policies. The school management reserves the right to modify these policies with prior notice. For any refund-related queries, please contact the school office.
